# Tile Garden engine contract

Tile puzzles keep `mechanic: "tile"`, row-major integer `cells`, `cols`, and `rows`. Optional `tileShape` is `"domino"` (the default when omitted) or `"l-tromino"`. Other values are rejected. Each puzzle uses one shape throughout; mixed menus and inventories are unsupported.

Boards and solution witnesses are arrays of cell arrays. A domino contains two side-adjacent cells. An L-tromino contains three distinct corners of a 2×2 block, in any order. All four rotations are legal. Every cell must belong to the region and lie within its dimensions; pieces cannot overlap. Completion accepts every exact cover, independently of the authored witness.

`tileSize`, `validTile`, and `tilePlacements` expose these rules. `move` accepts a complete cell array. `solveTiles(puzzle, board)` returns additional placements preserving existing pieces, `[]` for a complete cover, or `null` for an invalid board or dead end. `nextHint` retains its existing `pair` property, now containing either two or three cells. Removal lifts the entire piece containing the selected cell; undo restores the preceding board.

`dist/tile-controls.js` exposes DOM-free input rules for shared tap and stroke controls. `classifyTileCells` returns `extendable`, `complete`, or `blocked` against currently empty legal placements; it does not require the remaining board to be solvable. `tapTileSelection` toggles a selected cell or restarts an incompatible selection at the latest cell. `startTileStroke` and `extendTileStroke` keep distinct cells in entry order and retain a blocked result until release. `tileStrokeTarget` maps viewport coordinates to a grid cell only in its central 60%, inset 20% from each edge; ordinary gutters return `null`, and leaving the board returns `-1`. The caller supplies rectangles for every grid square, including holes. These helpers do not edit attempts or saves.

Content validation checks area divisibility by the selected tile size, solver feasibility, and every witness move through the same rules. Focused engine tests exercise all orientations, invalid geometry, alternate covers, partial states, dead ends, removal, undo, and legacy domino compatibility.

Pressing an empty cell starts a stroke with pointer capture. Dragging across empty cells previews the next tile color in place; a legal piece is submitted through `move` only on release. Revisiting a cell does not erase it. A stroke that becomes impossible shows the dashed blocked preview and stays blocked. An incomplete or blocked release, pointer cancellation, capture loss, or navigation places nothing and does not add a move or error text. A press and release in one cell acts as a tap. A second pointer is ignored. The board suppresses touch scrolling and zooming; the page still scrolls outside it.

Tapping cells in any order submits a complete piece on the third L tap or second domino tap. Tapping a selected cell unselects it; an incompatible tap starts a new selection without an error. Enter and Space on focused cell buttons use the same action and retain focus when that cell remains on the board. Tapping any occupied cell or the painted domino body lifts the entire piece. Each placed L paints only its three cells, with matching colors and tile numbers. Undo, restart, hints, completion, and saved board/history remain on the existing engine and storage paths.

The tap selection and active stroke live only in `dist/main.js` UI state. Preview classes change existing board nodes during the stroke; no render, move, history update, or save occurs until a legal release. A render discards a pending stroke. Neither transient selection nor pointer state is serialized. Both library play and campaign encounters render through the shared Tile Garden board. Its fixed piece picture has an accessible name; the only visible instruction, “Drag across 3 squares” or “Drag across 2 squares,” appears while the board is empty.

Help and read-aloud use the configured shape; solver hints highlight and describe every cell. The shipped pack now includes nine L-only replacements; see [authoring and migration notes](l-gardens.md).
