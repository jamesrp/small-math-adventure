# Every week to a decision

James asked on October 10, 2026 that every worksheet week end in one of two states: **in the app**, or **not porting**, with the reason written down. "Waiting" is a step in a wave, never an end state. A week reaches its decision through its review card in the worksheets repository ([plans/review](https://github.com/jamesrp/math-circle-worksheets/tree/main/plans/review)), and then a port when the card says port. A week that waits on a board or on another family gets that board or family built in a wave, or a reason why not.

**Where things stand:** 101 weeks (1–100 and the Week 1 encore). 81 are in the app and 5 are ruled out, so 15 are open: Weeks 84–87, 89 and 91–100, each with a Wave 10 port set by its card. Week 90 merged on October 23 as Sliding numbers, and Week 83 the same day as Stalks, a group in Pebble Duel. Every week from 83 to 100 had its card by October 23 (Week 88's rates it C, so it is not porting), and James gave full autonomy that day to carry the app forward. Week 81 merged on October 17 as the Buckets group in Pebble Duel. Wave 7 is done. James lifted the October 10 hold on Waves 8 and 9 on October 16 and asked for the remaining work to be split across threads of a few weeks each, so the 14 open weeks and the touch-ups became five batches (below), each in its own thread. All five merged on October 17, and every row here was checked against the cards, themes.md and the specs that day.

Whoever changes a week's state changes its row here in the same push, and the count above.
