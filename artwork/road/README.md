# Lantern Road artwork

Six new environment illustrations generated with the built-in ImageGen tool. The complete prompts and generated-source paths are recorded in `prompts.json`; selected original PNGs are in `originals/`. The source illustrations were inspected for scene clarity, consistent dusk palette, open foreground, and absence of UI/text or duplicate characters.

The runtime uses JPEG copies in `dist/assets/road/`, encoded with `sips` at quality 86 without compositing or repainting. The app overlays the existing vector companions and interactive progress in `dist/road-art.js`. Exact puzzle states remain on their mathematical boards. Old rescue artwork is retained for the historical campaign.
